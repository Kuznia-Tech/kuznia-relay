A Deviation is never a Pass

Where we chose differently from the client’s requirement, QA says so and the client signs it off. QA never fixes; fails become findings.

The second model is there because a model checking its own work tends to share its own blind spots. Deviations are listed separately so our engineering decisions never silently replace client requirements at sign-off.
