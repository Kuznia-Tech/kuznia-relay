# Code of Conduct

Kuznia Relay follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, give and take feedback gracefully, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through the [Kuznia-Tech organisation on GitHub](https://github.com/Kuznia-Tech). Reports are handled confidentially, and maintainers may remove comments, close issues or block contributors who break this code.
